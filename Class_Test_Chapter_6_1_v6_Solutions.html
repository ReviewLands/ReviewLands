১. অপনয়ন পদ্ধতি

(ক) 2x + y = 6, x + 2y = 6 → (2, 2)

(খ) 2x - y = 6, x - 2y = 6 → (2, -2)

(গ) ax + by = ab, bx + ay = ab

(i)+(ii): (a+b)(x+y)=2ab; (i)−(ii): (a-b)(x-y)=0 → x=y (a≠b)

∴ (x, y) = (ab/(a+b), ab/(a+b))

(খ) 5x - 2y = 3, 5x + 2y = 7 → (1, 1)

(গ) ax - by = a - b, ax + by = a + b → (1, 1)
